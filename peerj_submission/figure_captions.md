# Figure captions

**Figure 1. Reproducible paired-evaluation workflow.** Three seeds each supply 32 parent draws, reused across four transformed model families and 12 sensing scenarios. The 96 parent blocks each contain 48 paired trials per alternative. The frozen score is retrospective because realised baseline energy enters its features. Approximate seed-stratified parent-block bootstrap summaries and exploratory sign-flip tests are reported with declared Holm families. Final-time spatial endpoints, population sensitivity and conditional preference ranks are interpreted explicitly. The reproduction script builds all outputs in sequence. Plotting code was revised with OpenAI Codex (GPT-6); the image was rendered programmatically from the stated inputs.

**Figure 2. Paired success change by alternative and retrospective baseline-score band.** Points show controller-minus-baseline success changes; horizontal lines are approximate 95% percentile intervals from 10,000 seed-stratified parent-block bootstrap replicates. There are 96 blocks overall, 54 low-band, 81 intermediate-band and 72 high-band; a block can span bands. The vertical line marks zero. Bands use the same realised-baseline score for every alternative and are unavailable before treatment. Positive low-band estimates have intervals spanning zero and adjusted exploratory p-values of 1.000. These intervals do not reproduce the Latin-hypercube design-based sampling distribution. Plotting code was revised with OpenAI Codex (GPT-6); the image was rendered programmatically from the stated inputs.

**Figure 3. Final-time failure and energy changes.** Panels A–D show thermal failure, ROS failure, overheating and delivered energy, respectively, with approximate 95% seed-stratified parent-block bootstrap intervals. Endpoints are evaluated over radial nodes at the final simulation time. Thermal failure excludes cases failing the temperature criterion, so its decrease is not evidence of improved heating reliability. Hypoxic-failure changes are zero and omitted. Joint warm/safe counts are supplied in warm_safe_joint.csv. These are simulator endpoints rather than clinical injury measures. Plotting code was revised with OpenAI Codex (GPT-6); the image was rendered programmatically from the stated inputs.

**Figure 4. Point-estimate trade-offs and conditional ranks.** Panels A and B plot success change against final-time overheating and energy changes. Panel C gives the fraction ranking first under 200,000 Dirichlet(1,1,1,1,1) weight draws over the five min-max-normalised objectives. All four alternatives are non-dominated at their observed means; uncertainty in Pareto membership is not propagated. The baseline is the reference, not a fifth candidate. No alternative ranks first in more than half of the sampled weight vectors. Frequencies depend on the candidate set, objectives, normalisation and distribution of weights. Plotting code was revised with OpenAI Codex (GPT-6); the image was rendered programmatically from the stated inputs.

**Figure 5. Seed and scenario population sensitivity.** Panels A–D show Constrained optimized, smoothed predictive, thermal-floor predictive and reduced-order model predictive control. Each contains 16 specifications: four seed subsets crossed with four scenario subsets. Points are sorted by estimate, with approximate intervals from 2,000 parent-block resamples. A black-edged diamond marks the all-seed/all-scenario primary specification. Upward triangles denote positive estimates, downward triangles negative estimates and grey circles zero estimates; position also encodes sign. Positive fractions are 37.5%, 25%, 25% and 0%. Scenario restriction changes the target population. specification_plot_key.csv identifies each position, subset, estimate and interval. Plotting code was revised with OpenAI Codex (GPT-6); the image was rendered programmatically from the stated inputs.
